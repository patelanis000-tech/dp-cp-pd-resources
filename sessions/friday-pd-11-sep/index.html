Invigilation · preparation

Before the examination

  • Know exactly when and where you are invigilating.
  • Allow time for distribution and collection of examination materials.
  • Allow for the five-minute reading time where applicable.
  • Teachers of the subject being examined must not invigilate that examination.
  • For calculator examinations, know the IB calculator rules and how to check examination mode.
  • Ensure the room has a visible clock and displayed start/finish times.
  • Ensure unauthorized teaching material is removed from the room.
  • Ensure invigilators can contact someone outside the room in an emergency.
  • Keep a copy of the Conduct of examinations booklet available in the room.
Invigilation · students entering

When students arrive

  • Admit students at least 10 minutes before the examination starts.
  • Students enter quietly and orderly.
  • Only authorized materials may go to the desk.
  • Personal belongings, including communication devices, media players and wristwatches, must be left outside or at the back of the room.
  • Check student identity.
  • Check pencil cases or containers if they are allowed.
  • Be especially vigilant for unauthorized electronic devices.
  • Students must not share stationery, dictionaries, calculators or other materials.
Invigilation · starting

Starting the examination

  • Students must remain silent until they have left the examination room.
  • Ensure students have the correct coversheet, paper and required stationery/materials.
  • No rough/scratch paper is permitted.
  • Open sealed examination packets in the presence of students.
  • Make sure every student has the correct paper, subject, level and language.
  • Write the exact start and finish times where all students can see them.
  • Give the required five-minute reading time except for stand-alone multiple-choice papers.
  • During reading time: no writing, dictionaries, highlighting or calculators.
Invigilation · during

During the examination

  • Give your whole attention to supervising the examination.
  • Do not read, mark work, use a laptop for other work, or do anything unrelated to invigilation.
  • Conversations between invigilators must be brief, quiet and only about the examination.
  • At least one invigilator must walk around the room at regular intervals.
  • Do not stay in one place for long, but avoid distracting students.
  • One invigilator should be seated at the back of the room.
  • Never leave students unsupervised.
  • Keep an accurate record of significant events during the examination.
Invigilation · questions and incidents

Questions, illness and misconduct

  • If a student asks about the meaning of a question or thinks there is an error, tell them to follow the paper instructions and interpret the question for themselves.
  • Do not explain the question, suggest an interpretation or tell a student to ignore an instruction.
  • Record illness, temporary absence and suspected misconduct, including times.
  • A student leaving temporarily must be supervised and must not access or take examination material.
  • If misconduct is suspected, record the incident and normally allow the student to finish.
  • Disruptive behaviour must be stopped immediately.
Invigilation · leaving the room

Temporary absence and early departure

  • Students leaving temporarily must be supervised and may not communicate with anyone except the supervising person.
  • They must not take material out, access material while away, or return with material.
  • Students must not leave during the first hour or the last 15 minutes of an examination.
  • If a student leaves early, they must remain supervised until the required end of the supervision period.
  • If a student leaves before the scheduled finish time, they must not return to the examination.
Invigilation · ending

Ending the examination

  • Give the required time warnings.
  • At the end, tell students to stop writing immediately.
  • Students must not add to or amend answers after the examination ends.
  • Collect all examination papers, scripts, answer coversheets, MCQ sheets and unused materials before students leave.
  • Ensure students have completed coversheets and attached materials correctly.
  • Students leave quietly and may take only their personal belongings.
  • No examination paper, data booklet, answer booklet or graph paper may leave the room with a student.
Invigilation · after students leave

After the examination

  • Invigilators remain responsible for the secure handling of scripts until they are sealed.
  • Arrange scripts in numerical student order by session number.
  • Check that the correct coversheet, paper, answer booklet(s) and graph paper are present where required.
  • Do not photocopy scripts or MCQ answer sheets.
  • Secure examination papers and remaining stationery.
  • For calculator examinations, clear calculator memories at the end of the examination.
