Approaches to Learning

Social ATL

Group work does not automatically build social skills.

Teach the collaboration you want to see.

What are we actually teaching?

  • Listening to others
  • Building on ideas
  • Disagreeing respectfully
  • Sharing responsibility
  • Making decisions together

What can teachers do?

Use rotating group roles.
Give sentence stems for disagreement.
Ask students to paraphrase before responding.
Require every member to explain the group’s decision.
Pause midway: “How well are we working together?”
Assess the process as well as the final product.
Closing questionWhat is one social skill you could deliberately teach next time students work together?
Speaker notes
Keep this practical. Social ATL includes smaller teachable behaviours: listening, contributing, managing disagreement, sharing responsibility and making decisions. Give one quick classroom example: before students disagree, require them to paraphrase the previous speaker's point accurately. Emphasize that teachers can make the social process visible and assessable, not just the final group product.
