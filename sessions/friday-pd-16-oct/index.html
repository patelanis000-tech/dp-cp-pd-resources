Before the session

Arrival and room setup

Morning reading time

9:30 AM

First invigilators arrive at 9:15 AM.

Afternoon reading time

1:30 PM

First invigilators arrive at 1:15 PM.

  • Help prepare seating and materials. Make sure you can see all students.
  • Check the exam-clock software: correct session and times, visible to everyone.
  • Read the notices to candidates before the examination.
  • Anis brings sealed packets where possible. Otherwise collect them from him during setup, without leaving students unattended.

Keep packets sealed until opening them in front of the students and the other invigilator.

Entry to the room

Admission and identity

Exam conditions apply from entry.

  • Students arrive 15 minutes early in regular uniform.
  • Admit quietly at least ten minutes before the examination begins.
  • Check assigned seats and keep entry orderly.
  • Confirm every student’s identity. A school ID is not required to sit the exam.
  • Make sure you or another staff member can confirm identity.
  • Only permitted materials may be on desks.
Entry to the room

Belongings and desk checks

In the MPH, bags, phones and all wristwatches go in the designated spot on the bleachers at the side.

  • Have students switch off phones and watch alarms.
  • Give everyone an opportunity to declare unauthorized items.
  • Pencil cases must be transparent.
  • Water bottles must be clear and label-free.
  • No sharing stationery, dictionaries or calculators.
Before the paper

Materials and calculators

Materials checks

  • Inspect lucky charms.
  • Dictionaries must be inspected in advance.
  • Personal earplugs require approved arrangements.
  • The school supplies tissues.
  • Use the room clock. No individual desk clocks.

Calculators

Anis will send instructions for checking and preparing calculators. Follow those instructions and refer any uncertainty to him.

Starting the examination

Starting the paper

Check that every student has the correct paper and required materials, with the front cover facing up.

Read the full official IB instructions for that paper.

Give five minutes of reading time. Students must not write, highlight, use calculators or consult dictionaries.

Check the software’s start and finish times before writing begins.

Starting the examination

Listening examinations

Give five minutes of reading time before pressing play.

Whole-room audio

  • Do not pause or replay the audio.
  • Pauses in the recording are built in.

Standard delivery

  • Follow the approved arrangements.
  • Do not improvise access arrangements.
  • If audio has started when a student arrives, contact the coordinator.

Only after all listening exams have finished, students may gather in one room with two invigilators for the remaining hold. Maintain supervision during transfer and until the authorized release time.

During the examination

Active supervision

Attention and equipment

  • No marking, unrelated reading or other tasks.
  • Keep staff conversations minimal, quiet and exam-related.
  • No personal phone or computer use.
  • Use the exam computer only to run the clock or audio.

Movement and supervision

  • Walk regularly without disturbing students.
  • Keep a good view of everyone.
  • Do not lean over students to read answers or comment on their work.
  • Never leave students unsupervised.
During the examination

Requests, concerns and records

Practical requests

You may address requests for extra paper or bathroom permission.

Questions about content

Do not explain, interpret or answer them. Record the student’s concern and refer it to the coordinator.

Incident records

Record what you observed, with the student’s identity and the relevant times.

Keep the account factual.

During the examination

Bathroom visits

One invigilator accompanies the student to the toilet. The other stays in the exam room. For the three-room listening arrangement, a floater accompanies the student.

Record departure and return. Check the bathroom for unauthorized materials before and after each use.

No unauthorized materials and no communication with anyone except the supervising invigilator.

Refer illness or extra-time requests to the coordinator.
An ordinary bathroom visit does not automatically earn extra time.

During the examination

Late arrivals

Allow the student to start promptly with full examination time.

Ordinary late arrival

  • Do not wait for coordinator permission.
  • Record the arrival and inform the coordinator for reporting.

Two situations to refer

  • Others in the group have finished: the coordinator decides admission based on security.
  • Listening audio has started: contact the coordinator for arrangements. Do not replay or restart.
Between papers

Breaks between consecutive papers

Within the same morning or afternoon session, supervision continues throughout the break.

  • Keep all students supervised.
  • Supervise bathroom visits during the break.
  • No talking if other examinations are still taking place in the room.

This rule does not refer to lunch between the morning and afternoon sessions.

If something goes wrong

Suspected misconduct

Record what you observe and inform the coordinator discreetly.

Keep the account factual, with identities and relevant times.

Let the student finish unless disruptive behaviour requires intervention.

Maintain supervision of the rest of the room.

If something goes wrong

Emergencies and evacuation

Prioritize safety. Maintain supervision and examination security.

If the room is evacuated

  • Students must not communicate.
  • Record stop and restart times.
  • Preserve remaining examination time.

Decisions and records

  • Contact the coordinator. Record what happened and the relevant times.
  • Do not independently authorize extra time or rescheduling.
At the end of the paper

Finishing and collecting materials

Tell students to stop writing answers immediately when time is up.

When instructed, students may complete administrative names/details on answer booklets and cover sheets, as applicable. They must not add to or change answers.

Collect and account for all exam materials, including unused materials, while students remain seated.

Keep students supervised until authorized to leave.
No exam materials may leave with students.

Checks before attachment

  • Count only four-page answer booklets. Exclude individual pages, graph paper and other sheets.
  • Put the name and session number on the front of the answer booklet.
  • For graph paper, write “see graph paper” beside the relevant answer in the answer booklet.
  • Students leave the invigilator-only absence box untouched.
  • Attach the work with a string tag.
After the examination

The two-hour supervision rule

2hours

Minimum supervision
from exam start

It is not two additional hours after finishing.

  • If a short paper ends earlier, continue supervision.
  • No phones, internet or communication devices during the hold.
  • Before release, account for all exam materials and check that at least two hours from exam start have elapsed.
  • Keep students supervised until the authorized release time after all papers have finished.
After the examination

Handover and confidentiality

Secure handover

  • Keep scripts secure.
  • Hand exam materials and incident records to Anis.
  • Do not copy examination materials.

Confidentiality

Keep exam content confidential outside the immediate cohort for 24 hours after the exam ends.

Do not distribute exam materials.
